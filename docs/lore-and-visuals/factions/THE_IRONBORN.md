# The Ironborn

**Full name:** The Ironborn Guild  
**Ideology:** Industry, craft, pragmatic meritocracy — results over origin  
**Alignment:** Lawful  
**Home biomes:** Mountains, badlands, volcanic  
**Color:** #8b4513 (iron-brown, warm dark)  
**Symbol:** A hammer over an anvil  

## Who they are

The Ironborn are not an ethnic group — they are a professional identity.
Anyone can be Ironborn if they can work metal and uphold the Guild Charter.
This inclusivity is their source of cultural pride and also their biggest
political asset: they cannot be accused of tribalism the way the Free
Holds or the Accord sometimes can.

They have a deep, complicated relationship with their own history. The
Ashborn (their ancestor culture) are blamed by the Accord for the Ruin.
The Ironborn's official position is that this is "era-II politics that do
not reflect the Guild's values or its responsibility." Their unofficial
position, which emerges in late-game dialogue, is more complicated.

The Great Smelter of Ashenmoor (E1Y214) is their holy site in everything
but name. An Ironborn NPC who takes you to the Smelter ruins and explains
their history is one of the game's best character moments (planned content,
not yet built).

## What they want from the player

- Prove you can work. The Ironborn do not give favors based on who you
  know; they give them based on what you've made.
- Early Ironborn quests are mechanical/craft-focused: bring a quota of
  refined materials, repair a broken machine (once the machine system
  exists), find a specific ore vein and report its coordinates.
- Later quests involve the Coal Compact (their political independence),
  Accord overreach, and the deep question of whether their ancestor
  culture caused the Ruin and what (if anything) they owe for that.

## Two starter quests for the Ironborn

**Quest ID: `ironborn_q1_ore_survey`**  
Title: "The Vein Finder"  
Issuing NPC: An Ironborn Foreman in an `ironborn_forge_camp` structure  
Objective type: `Collect` (bring 20 iron ore)  
Narrative: "We don't give Guild standing to wanderers who talk well. Bring
us 20 iron ore — raw, not smelted. We'll smelt it ourselves. That's how
we know you can find ore, not just buy it."  
Reward: +15 Ironborn, access to the forge_camp's furnace (can use it freely
after), 3 iron ingots.  
Standing consequence: +15 Ironborn only.

**Quest ID: `ironborn_q2_machine_parts`**  
Title: "Parts for the Press"  
Issuing NPC: Same Foreman  
Objective type: `Craft` (craft 5 iron plates — a new recipe added as
part of this quest, 3 iron ingots + 1 coal → 5 iron plates in the furnace)  
Narrative: "The old press needs new faceplates. Iron, smelted clean, and
cut flat. You know the recipe now. Bring me five."  
Reward: +15 Ironborn, the iron plate recipe permanently added to the
player's recipe book, 2 coal, 1 iron pick.  
Standing consequence: +15 Ironborn only.

## Companion type available from this faction (at standing ≥ +75)

**Ironborn Artisan** — a stocky NPC in Ironborn-brown leathers with a
tool belt. Skill set: crafting (can use the furnace/crusher/assembler
autonomously), mining. The best companion for automated production tasks.
Daily wage: 6 iron ingots or 3 coal.
