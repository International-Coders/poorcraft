# The Ember Covenant

**Full name:** The Ember Covenant  
**Ideology:** Balance of Anima (magic), healing the world's Ruin-wounds,
ecological stewardship  
**Alignment:** Neutral  
**Home biomes:** Highland forest, mushroom forest, bog/swamp  
**Color:** #c4602a (ember-orange, warm)  
**Symbol:** A flame inside a circle of leaves  

## Who they are

The Covenant is not a religion, though it sometimes looks like one. They
are a knowledge order focused on Anima (magic) — studying it, teaching it,
and using it to heal ecological damage from the Ruin. They plant things
where the Ruin scorched the earth. They maintain the bog forests. They
tend the mushroom forests that grew during the Long Winter.

They have real power: they are the only faction with functional, teachable
magic. Every other faction either wants to control that (the Accord), is
wary of it (the Ironborn), or wants to learn it quietly (the Ashen Order).

In Era IV, they have rediscovered a fragment of pre-Ruin magic — something
more powerful than their normal teaching-level spells. They are being very
careful about this. Some Covenant members want to share it openly; others
want to study it in secret first. This internal fracture is a plot thread
available to a high-standing Covenant player.

## Two starter quests for the Covenant

**Quest ID: `covenant_q1_healing_herbs`**  
Title: "What the Bog Gives"  
Issuing NPC: A Covenant Herbalist at a `covenant_grove_shrine`  
Objective type: `Collect` (gather 10 bog-grass or swamp-biome plant items)  
Narrative: "The shrine's healing stores are low. The bog gives freely to
those who know where to look — and to those who approach it without fear.
Bring me ten bundles of what you find there."  
Reward: +15 Covenant, a "Minor Healing" potion recipe, 3 food items.

**Quest ID: `covenant_q2_ember_stone`**  
Title: "Tend the Ember Stone"  
Issuing NPC: Same Herbalist  
Objective type: `Reach` (find and visit 3 `ember_glowstone` natural
formations in the highland biome) + `Craft` (place an ember_glowstone
block near each formation)  
Narrative: "The ember stones mark where Anima concentrates in the highland.
We tend them — not to extract their power, but to remind the world we're
watching. Place one of our blessed stones near each natural formation."  
Reward: +15 Covenant, the ember_glowstone crafting recipe, access to the
Covenant's spell-teaching services at the shrine (once the magic system
from the main roadmap is built).

## Companion type available from this faction (at standing ≥ +75)

**Covenant Channeler** — slender NPC in dark-wood-toned robes with ember-
orange trim. Skill set: magic support (casts a basic ward on the player
during combat), herb/resource gathering, can read lore books aloud as
contextual dialogue. Daily wage: 4 food items + 1 Anima crystal (a new
item, crafted from ember_glowstone + coal).
