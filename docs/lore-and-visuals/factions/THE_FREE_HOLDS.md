# The Free Holds

**Full name:** The Free Holds  
**Ideology:** Land independence, traditional craft, deep suspicion of
centralized authority in any form  
**Alignment:** Neutral  
**Home biomes:** Savanna, coastal, open plains (rural and frontier)  
**Color:** #6b8e23 (earthy green, land-toned)  
**Symbol:** Three bound wheat stalks  

## Who they are

The Free Holds are not a government — they are a cultural identity.
Settlements that call themselves Free Holds share a common thread: they
did not sign the Accord of Ashenmoor and do not recognize its authority
over their land. Some are prosperous farming communities that simply
want to be left alone. Some are rough frontier outposts with no patience
for "Ashenmoor's paper rules." Some are former Accord settlements that
broke away after a specific grievance.

They are unified by what they reject more than what they embrace, which
makes them politically incoherent but culturally vivid. A Free Holds
longhouse is a real place with people who have opinions about their
crops, their neighbors, and their particular local history.

They are not isolationist — they trade with the Ironborn extensively,
tolerate the Covenant when the Covenant tends land near their settlements,
and have a wary but functional relationship with the Ashen Order (who
they respect as "people who actually keep records, not just issue
declarations"). What they won't do is take orders.

## Two starter quests for the Free Holds

**Quest ID: `freeholds_q1_harvest`**  
Title: "Hands on the Harvest"  
Issuing NPC: A Free Holds Elder at a `freeholds_longhouse`  
Objective type: `Collect` (bring 20 food items, any type)  
Narrative: "I don't care what the Accord says you are. If you can bring
twenty good harvests, you're useful. We've been short this season and
I'd rather have a stranger's help than an Accord official's permission."  
Reward: +15 Free Holds, 5 bread/food items, access to the longhouse's
crafting table and chest storage.  
Standing consequence: +15 Free Holds, –3 Accord (word gets around that
you're doing Free Holds favors).

**Quest ID: `freeholds_q2_boundary_markers`**  
Title: "Our Ground, Our Markers"  
Issuing NPC: Same Elder  
Objective type: `Craft` + `Place` (craft 5 `freeholds_daub` boundary
markers — a simple recipe — and place them at 5 marked boundary points)  
Narrative: "The Accord keeps redrawing maps and calling our land
'unclaimed territory.' It's not unclaimed. Place these markers at the
boundary so any cartographer who walks through knows this is Free Holds
ground. I don't care if the Accord argues — the stones will be there."  
Reward: +15 Free Holds, –5 Accord (they notice boundary markers placed
against their territorial claims), 3 food items, a Free Holds crafting
recipe for `freeholds_thatch` building blocks.

## Companion type available from this faction (at standing ≥ +75)

**Free Holds Scout** — wiry NPC in earthy-green practical clothes, carries
a bow. Skill set: ranged combat, tracking (highlights mob positions in a
small radius on the minimap while following), foraging (collects food
passively from plants passed while following). Daily wage: 5 food items.
