# Asset Catalog Brainstorm

This is a starter catalog for Z-code to expand. Each row must become a gameplay
object, not a decorative mesh.

## Survival And Settlement

- starter shelter with openable door, bed anchor, chest anchor, smoke vent;
- woodcutter cabin with stump work area, log pile, axe rack, NPC job anchor;
- farm plot with growth states, water need, harvest action, scare marker;
- tavern with seats, rumor board, trader NPC, rented bed, fire hazard state;
- stable with animal pen, gate latch, feed trough, mount spawn anchor;
- market stall with trade anchor, owner schedule, stock crates, price sign;
- healer hut with herb storage, bed, potion station, injured NPC slot.

## Industry And Magic

- forge with heat, bellows, anvil, recipe input, output tray, danger radius;
- kiln with fuel, clay input, temperature, chimney smoke, cracked failure;
- water wheel with river alignment, torque output, broken paddle state;
- steam boiler with pressure, relief valve, pipe ports, explosion warning;
- crystal altar with rune sockets, mana draw, ritual progress, corruption;
- research bench with paper slots, lens, experiment state, unlock output;
- valve computer cabinet with tube slots, heat, signal output, maintenance.

## Exploration And Combat

- ruined watchtower with climb path, loot cache, line-of-sight marker;
- cave shrine with offering slot, trap trigger, lore plaque, lighting state;
- monster nest with spawn rule, damaged state, loot, faction alert radius;
- bandit camp with patrol anchors, alarm bell, prisoner cage, loot chest;
- bridge checkpoint with toll NPC, gate, faction banner, siege damage state;
- battlefield marker with corpse loot, morale aura, chronicle event.

## Strategy Layer

- town hall marker with population, taxes, requests, militia, build queue;
- faction banner with ownership, contested state, diplomacy tooltip;
- road node with route weight, caravan traffic, danger score;
- mine claim with ore type, depth, workers, storage, depletion;
- siege camp with supply, morale, engines, attack vector.

## UI And HUD Assets

- health/stamina/food/air/mana bars with alpha, warning, depleted states;
- hotbar frames with selected, disabled, cooldown, durability overlays;
- keycaps generated from bindings, never baked text;
- tool icons for build, mine, talk, trade, forge, map, inspect, screenshot;
- resource icons for wood, stone, ore, food, mana, power, population, gold.
