# Survival, Combat, And Exploration

The first-person loop must feel immediate and readable.

## Survival

- health, stamina, food, thirst optional, temperature, sleep, injury;
- shelter, fire, weather, clothing/armor, medicine;
- danger warnings that are visible in HUD and audio later.

## Combat

- melee, bow, magic, shield, armor, dodge, stamina cost, knockback;
- enemy tells, weak points, loot, XP, injuries, fleeing/surrender;
- faction consequences for violence.

## Exploration

- caves, ruins, towers, dungeons, camps, ships, portals, monuments;
- climbing/ladders/ropes, swimming/boats, mounts/roads/fast travel later;
- secrets with readable clues, not random invisible triggers.

## Bosses And Events

- regional threats, siege events, monster nests, faction raids, weather
  disasters, magical anomalies;
- events must have warning, response options, and aftermath.

## Proofs

- player can complete a short route: gather, craft, fight, heal, return;
- combat log matches HP/stamina changes;
- dungeon entrance is discoverable and traversable;
- reward appears in inventory and observatory exports.
