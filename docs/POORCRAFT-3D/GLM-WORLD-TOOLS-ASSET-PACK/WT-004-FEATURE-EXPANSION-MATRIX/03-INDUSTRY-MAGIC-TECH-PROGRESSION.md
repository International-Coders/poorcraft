# Industry, Magic, And Tech Progression

Progression must support freedom: player-built machines, magic paths, hybrid
solutions, automation, and visible consequences.

## Early Industry

- hand tools, workbench, campfire, furnace, forge, anvil, kiln, sawmill;
- recipes with inputs, outputs, time, fuel, skill, tool tier;
- visible state: hot, cold, blocked, working, complete, broken.

## Power And Automation

- water wheel, windmill, belts, shafts, pipes, pumps, boiler, generator,
  battery, switchboard, valve computer, sensors;
- power networks with generation, storage, loss, overload, and safety;
- machine UI and in-world indicators.

## Magic

- mana, rituals, runes, crystals, altars, familiars, wards, teleport anchors;
- corruption, backlash, cooldowns, environmental cost;
- magic can solve some industry problems differently, not just duplicate.

## Research

- discoveries from exploration, NPC teachers, books, experiments, ruins;
- parallel research branches: craft, combat, farming, magic, machines,
  governance, navigation;
- failures teach, but never brick a save.

## Proofs

- forge route consumes ore/fuel and creates output;
- generator powers a machine;
- overloaded network reports a reason;
- rune effect has observable state;
- research unlock changes available recipes/UI.
