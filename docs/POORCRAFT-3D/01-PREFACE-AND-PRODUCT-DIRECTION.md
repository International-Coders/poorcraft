# POORCRAFT 3D — Product Preface

POORCRAFT 3D is a voxel sandbox RPG and empire-building game where the player
can shape an entire world and decide what kind of civilization they want to
create.

The game is inspired by the breadth and freedom of large Minecraft modpacks
such as All the Mods, but it is an original game with its own engine, lore,
factions, assets, and systems. Players should be able to specialize deeply in
different directions:

- industrial technology, machines, computers, electricity, steam, and nuclear
  systems;
- magic, dragons, enchanted tools, rituals, and supernatural civilizations;
- exploration, combat, fishing, crafting, building, and survival;
- diplomacy, conquest, faction politics, castles, armies, and empire
  management.

The player is not restricted to being a crafter who builds machines forever.
They may begin as a lone survivor, adventurer, mage, engineer, mercenary, or
ruler. Over time, they can establish a settlement, attract and organize NPCs,
construct castles, control resources, form alliances, conquer territory, and
develop an empire.

## Three connected scales

### Personal scale

Exploration, combat, tools, magic, crafting, building, fishing, survival, and
relationships with individual characters.

### Settlement scale

NPC jobs, homes, workshops, farms, defenses, production chains, faction
identity, local events, laws, alarms, and visible construction.

### Empire scale

Castles, territories, economies, armies, diplomacy, strategic resources,
rival factions, roads, trade, and large-scale conflict.

These layers must affect one another. A river can power machines. Machines can
support a castle. A castle can protect a settlement. NPCs can operate the
settlement. The settlement can become part of an empire. Magic, technology,
geography, and politics should create consequences in the same world.

## Product promise

Start with one character, shape the land, build a society, choose a path of
power, and decide what kind of empire the world remembers.

## Originality rule

The references describe qualities, not content to reproduce. POORCRAFT 3D
must not copy protected names, layouts, quests, creatures, visual assets, UI,
text, music, progression tables, or code. All factions, lore, structures,
recipes, systems, and art shipped by POORCRAFT 3D must be original or properly
licensed.

## What the player should repeatedly notice

The game should make the player look outward. A visible river should suggest a
mill, fish, travel, irrigation, danger, or a settlement site. Smoke from a
castle should suggest a forge district or trouble. A closed gate should imply
laws and people who can change their mind. A mountain should offer a route,
cave, resource, shrine, fortress site, or dragon territory.

The intended feeling is not “I have opened the next menu.” It is “I understand
why this place matters, and I have an idea for what to do here.”

## Reference use

The desired modpack-like breadth means deep optional systems that can cross
connect. It does not mean throwing hundreds of machines or spells at the
player at once. The desired strategy influence means strong realms and map
consequences. It does not mean leaving first-person play behind. The desired
RPG influence means situated characters and choices. It does not mean copying
another game's story structure.
