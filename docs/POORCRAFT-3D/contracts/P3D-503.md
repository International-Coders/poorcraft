## Goal

Hostile creatures exist as entities with deterministic melee combat
(ranges, cooldowns, damage), drop LOOT as items on death, and a
procedural dungeon ROOM (a carved underground chamber connected by a
corridor) provides the first danger loop — all on the fixed clock.

## Scope

### Must change

- `pc3d_world::combat`:
  - `Creature { entity, pos, hp, max_hp, damage, attack_cooldown,
    aggro_radius }`; `CreatureSystem` holding creatures.
  - Deterministic melee: `creature_attack` hits the player when within
    range 1 and cooldown expired (cooldown 30 ticks, damage per
    creature); `player_attack(gen, creatures, pos, tool) -> Loot` hits
    the creature within range 1; death yields LOOT (item stacks).
  - `DungeonRoom::carve(gen, center) -> (cells to clear as Air, floor
    cells)`: a bounded rectangular chamber + corridor carved UNDERGROUND
    (below the surface crust), deterministic per (seed, center).
- Tests: creature melee damage/cooldown exactness; player kill → loot
  drops match the loot table; creature death removes it; dungeon carve
  is deterministic, bounded, and underground; player-in-dungeon
  final_solid answers Air inside, Solid at walls (via overrides in the
  model, documenting the P3D-205 composition path).

### Explicit non-goals

- No AI pathing for creatures (they hold position; chase is later), no
  ranged attacks, no boss, no meshes.

## Contracts and invariants

- deterministic behavior: pure functions of state + tick.
- authority/ownership: `pc3d_world::combat` owns the loop.
- performance: O(creatures).
- player-visible result: fight a creature, take its loot, explore a
  carved room.
