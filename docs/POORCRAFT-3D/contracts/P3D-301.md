## Goal

From the seed's macro elevation field, a deterministic watershed is
derived: every region drains to exactly one lower neighbor (steepest
descent, deterministic tie-break), discharge accumulates downstream, and
edges carrying meaningful discharge form the RIVER GRAPH — pure, cycle-
free, seed-reproducible, and visibly drawn on the atlas.

## Current truth

- `pc3d_world::gen` has the continuous elevation field and macro fields
  (P3D-103); the atlas proof renders biome maps (P3D-104). No water
  structures exist.

## Scope

### Must change

- `pc3d_world::hydro`:
  - `RiverGraph::new(seed, half_regions)`: 8-neighborhood steepest-
    descent flow directions over region elevations (tie-break: lowest
    neighbor by (elev, x, z)); downstream map for every region; discharge
    = 1 + Σ upstream discharges, accumulated in descending elevation
    order; river edges = discharge ≥ RIVER_THRESHOLD (16).
  - `downstream(r) -> Option<RegionCoord>` (None at sinks),
    `discharge(r) -> u64`, `is_river(r) -> bool`, `river_edges()`.
  - `wetness(r) -> u8`: humidity + river-corridor bonus (D-016: rivers
    create wetter corridors), decaying with distance from the nearest
    river edge.
- Atlas: `--atlas` draws river edges as blue over the biome colors
  (visible watershed proof).
- Tests: determinism (same seed → identical graph); acyclicity (every
  downstream chain terminates at a sink within the grid); discharge
  conservation (each node's discharge = 1 + Σ discharges of regions
  flowing into it); seed sensitivity; river presence (edges exist in
  most seeds, and rivers flow strictly downhill); wetness higher near
  rivers than far from them.

### Explicit non-goals

- No per-cell rivers/carving (P3D-302 flow records refine representation;
  local rebuild P3D-303), no water rendering/simulation, no lakes
  (P3D-305 if needed).

## Contracts and invariants

- deterministic behavior: flow directions and discharge are pure
  functions of (seed, region set) — no iteration-order dependence.
- authority/ownership: `pc3d_world::hydro` owns the watershed; water
  consumers (machines, fishing, NPCs) query it.
- save/load behavior: derivable from seed — never stored.
- network behavior: n/a.
- performance budget: O(regions log regions) for the accumulation; a
  129×129-lattice graph builds in milliseconds.
- player-visible result: rivers the player can follow are real geographic
  features on the map, not decoration.
