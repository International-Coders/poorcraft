## Goal

Relationship contracts between the player's faction and other factions: Allied (mutual defense), Puppet (autonomous growth, tribute), Protectorate (protected, tribute), Rival (competing), and Conquered (captured, grows as puppet until released). Each has different autonomy, tribute, and growth behavior — all deterministic.

## Scope

- `pc3d_world::relationships`:
  - `RelationshipKind` (Allied/Puppet/Protectorate/Rival/Conquered) with per-kind autonomy, tribute rate, and growth multiplier.
  - `CityRelationship { faction, kind, autonomy: u8, prosperity: i64, population: i64 }`.
  - `RelationshipSystem` — hold relationships, `establish/release/change_kind`, `grow_puppets(days)`, `collect_tribute(faction) -> u64`.
- Tests: kind transitions, puppet growth over days, tribute collection, release, determinism.
