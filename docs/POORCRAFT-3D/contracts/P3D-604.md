## Goal

The economic engine: workshops produce goods deterministically, settlements trade surplus for deficit goods, population consumes food and goods driving demand, and prosperity tracks economic health. All integer, all pure.

## Scope

- `pc3d_world::economy`:
  - `Production { workshops: u32, output_per_workshop: i64 }` →
    `produce(workshops, tick) -> i64` goods.
  - `TradeRoute { from_id, to_id, goods: u64 }` — surplus settlement
    sends goods to deficit settlement.
  - `Consumption { population, food_per_capita }` → `consume(pop) -> i64`
    food eaten per day.
  - `EconomicState { goods, food, prosperity }` — evolves per day from
    production, consumption, and trade effects.
- Tests: production determinism, trade conservation, consumption drain,
  prosperity tracking, multi-settlement trade network.
