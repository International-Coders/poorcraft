## Goal

The oversight panel queries REAL settlement data — population, food,
defense, projects, garrison readiness, construction progress — and
aggregates it into a single summary for the player. No fake numbers:
every metric reads from the actual systems (settlement, garrison,
economy, construction).

## Scope

- `pc3d_world::oversight`:
  - `OversightSummary` with all metrics from real data sources.
  - `OversightPanel::query(gen, settlement_agg, garrison, economy) ->
    OversightSummary`.
  - Health score: composite of food sufficiency, defense, prosperity.
- Tests: metrics derive from real underlying state, health score
  composite, zero-state handling.
