## Goal

LOD selection over the interest tiers is defined and testable — every
patch maps to exactly one detail level by distance — and the seam law is
proven: neighboring patches agree EXACTLY at shared borders (identical
surface heights and materials at the same world positions) at every LOD
boundary, so no LOD transition can open a visual or collision gap.

## Current truth

- `pc3d_world::stream` has interest tiers (P3D-105); `gen` has one
  continuous surface function and shared carving (P3D-103/203); extraction
  candidates sample it per column (P3D-201). 93 pc3d tests green.

## Scope

### Must change

- `pc3d_world::lod`:
  - `LodLevel { Full, Mid, Far }` with configurable distance bands
    (defaults 0–96 / 96–320 / 320–1024 m, matching P3D-105 tiers).
  - `lod_for(viewer, patch_center) -> LodLevel` — pure distance mapping.
  - `seam_signature(gen, patch, axis, side) -> u64` — FNV hash of the
    effective surface heights + material codes along one border column
    strip; two neighbors sharing a border produce IDENTICAL signatures.
  - `border_agrees(gen, patch_a, patch_b, axis) -> bool`.
- Tests: lod_for monotonic + band boundaries exact; every neighbor pair
  across a 9×9 patch grid agrees on both axes for 4 seeds (the no-gap
  proof at query level); signatures byte-deterministic; LOD transitions
  do not affect seam agreement (the law is LOD-independent).

### Explicit non-goals

- No mesh generation/visual rendering (P3D-201 measured the solid query;
  visual meshing is the renderer stage). No transvoxel geometry — the
  seam contract proven here is what a future mesher must satisfy.

## Contracts and invariants

- deterministic behavior: signatures are pure functions of (seed, patch,
  axis).
- authority/ownership: `pc3d_world::lod` owns LOD selection; meshers
  consume, never redefine.
- save/load: n/a. network: n/a.
- performance budget: seam check O(border cells) = O(16).
- player-visible result: no visible cracks between near and far terrain —
  release-blocking defect made impossible at the query level.
