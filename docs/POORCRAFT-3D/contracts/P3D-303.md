## Goal

When edits change a region's elevation, the watershed rebuilds LOCALLY:
regions whose flow records actually changed get their revision bumped,
while regions whose records are unchanged keep their revision untouched —
an edit cannot rewrite the whole river map, and the rebuilt graph stays
acyclic and discharge-conserved.

## Current truth

- P3D-301/302: RiverGraph + FlowTable derived from macro elevation;
  revision starts at 1 with bump_revision (all-at-once). No override or
  dirty-region logic.

## Scope

### Must change

- `hydro::rebuild_with_overrides(gen, half, overrides) -> RiverGraph`:
  region elevation = macro elevation + override delta (clamped to the
  elevation range); the full steepest-descent/accumulation pipeline runs
  over the overridden field.
- `flow::FlowTable::from_graph_with_revisions(previous, graph)`: records
  derived fresh; a region whose new record is semantically equal
  (direction, slope, discharge, capacity) KEEPS its previous revision;
  a changed region's revision increments (or starts at 1 when no
  previous record exists). Table revision increments by 1 per rebuild.
- Tests: an override big enough to flip a region's downstream direction
  changes exactly that direction (and its revision) while far regions
  keep revisions; a small override changes nothing (no revision churn);
  rebuilt graph passes acyclicity + conservation; determinism.

### Explicit non-goals

- No per-cell rebuild (region granularity); no water simulation; no
  meshing.

## Contracts and invariants

- deterministic behavior: overrides are exact inputs; same overrides →
  same rebuilt graph and revisions.
- authority/ownership: rebuild logic lives in hydro/flow.
- save/load behavior: the table persists as in P3D-302; revision history
  beyond current is not stored.
- network behavior: n/a.
- performance budget: O(regions log regions) — the same as a full build
  (macro granularity is cheap; per-cell dirty tracking arrives with the
  mesher).
- player-visible result: dam up a valley and the river reroutes —
  locally, with everything else untouched.

## Verification

- tests: direction flip under override; revision churn isolation;
  acyclicity + conservation on rebuilds; determinism.
- smoke: `make p3d-smoke` green.
