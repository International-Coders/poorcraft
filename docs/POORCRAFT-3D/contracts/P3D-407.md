## Goal

Distant settlements simulate as AGGREGATES — population, food, defense,
prosperity scalars that grow/decay deterministically per simulated day —
while the settlement nearest the player runs FULL simulation. Reconciliation
PROMOTES the nearest aggregate to full simulation when the player nears
(preserving its scalar state as starting conditions) and DEMOTES when the
player leaves (folding full state back into scalars). Deterministic.

## Scope

### Must change

- `pc3d_world::settlement`:
  - `Aggregate { population: i64, food: i64, defense: i64,
    prosperity: i64 }` with `simulate_day`: food decays by population,
    population grows/shrinks by food surplus/shortage, defense decays,
    prosperity tracks food+defense — all integer, clamped at 0.
  - `Settlement { id, center: RegionCoord, name, state: SettlementState
    (Aggregate | Full{npc_ids: Vec<EntityId>}), aggregate: Aggregate }`.
  - `Settlements::new(seed, gen, half)` — deterministic sites (river
    regions, spaced by minimum Chebyshev distance via the hydro graph),
    one per ~24 regions.
  - `nearest_to(player_region)`, `promote(settlement, npc_ids)` /
    `demote(settlement, aggregate_out)` — state transitions preserving
    scalar state.
- Tests: sites deterministic + spaced; simulate_day conservation/growth
  rules; promote/demote preserve aggregates; exactly one Full settlement
  at a time (the near/far reconciliation invariant); determinism.

### Explicit non-goals

- No actual NPC spawning into the registry (P3D-404's registry will host
  them when content lands); no buildings/pathing; no economy beyond the
  four scalars.

## Contracts and invariants

- deterministic behavior: sites and daily evolution are pure functions.
- authority/ownership: `pc3d_world::settlement` owns settlement data.
- save/load: plain data (persist via framing later with the registry).
- performance: O(settlements) per simulated day.
- player-visible result: distant towns keep living (scalars move), and
  arriving at one hands over a consistent state.
