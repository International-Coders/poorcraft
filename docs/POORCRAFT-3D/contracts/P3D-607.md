## Goal

The settlement can raise and manage a GARRISON: recruit soldiers from
population, assign them to defense, track garrison strength, and manage
supply. The strategic layer exposes garrison strength and readiness as
queryable data.

## Scope

- `pc3d_world::garrison`:
  - `Garrison { region, soldiers, max_soldiers, supply, morale }`.
  - `recruit(population, soldiers, count) -> recruited` bounded by
    population and max_soldiers.
  - `supply_decay(soldiers, supply) -> supply` — soldiers consume supply.
  - `morale` affected by supply and pay.
  - `readiness(soldiers, supply, morale) -> u8` 0–100.
- Tests: recruit bounded, supply decay, morale, readiness, determinism.
