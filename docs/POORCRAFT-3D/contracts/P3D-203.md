## Scope

### Must change

- `gen::effective_surface_mm(wx, wz)`: the analytic surface plus CLIFF
  terracing — in rare noise-masked bands (60 m mask cells, mask > 0.58),
  the surface quantizes to 4 m steps, producing real cliff faces on
  hillsides. `final_solid`-consumers (regenerate, extraction candidates)
  all use the effective surface.
- `gen::is_carved(wx, wy, wz, depth_mm) -> bool`: 3D cave density via two
  intersecting mid-band 3D value-noise fields (worm-like voids), gated:
  only cells at least 4 m below the surface (a solid crust), never below
  y < 0 (WATER SEAL — oceans cannot drain into caves until hydrology
  lands, P3D-301), never deeper than 120 m (solid deep crust).
- `gen::carve(material, wx, wy, wz, depth_mm)`: shared carving step —
  `regenerate_patch` AND `final_solid` both apply it, so their agreement
  stays structural (P3D-202's guarantee survives).
- Extraction candidates (P3D-201) extract against the effective surface;
  scene pins re-probed where terracing shifted the surface; a real
  CLIFF scene joins the bake-off.
- Tests: cave existence (carved cells occur in scanned worlds); carving
  constraints (never above the effective surface + crust, never below
  y = 0, never beyond the deep crust); agreement test still green (the
  carved world is the same world for every consumer); cliff scene real
  (a pinned patch exhibits ≥ 4 m of terraced step inside its columns);
  determinism of all new fields.

### Explicit non-goals

- No meshing of caves (renderer stage), no cave decoration/spawn logic,
  no hydrology interaction (P3D-301 owns water-in-caves), no LOD.

## Contracts and invariants

- deterministic behavior: all new fields are pure lattice noise from the
  seed; same seed + coords → same carving everywhere.
- authority/ownership: carving lives in `gen`; `final_solid` and
  `regenerate_patch` share it — the single-answer law (P3D-202) holds.
- save/load behavior: untouched patches still regenerate identically
  (hashes will change vs loop 372 — acceptable: generator version
  advanced; the world meta/format law (P3D-002) is the versioning path).
- network behavior: n/a.
- performance budget: `is_carved` is 2 trilinear 3D noise evaluations
  (8 lattice hashes each) per UNDERGROUND cell candidate; patch
  regeneration must stay under the 16 ms budget (release; debug 8×).
- player-visible result: hillsides with real cliff faces and caves to
  explore underground — the D-005 requirement becomes real.
