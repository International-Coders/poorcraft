## Goal

A learnable rune magic system: the player learns RUNES, spends MANA
(regenerating on the fixed clock) to CAST world-facing effects (a Lumen
light placed at a target cell, a Dig rune that clears a cell), all
deterministic — the first magical progression path.

## Scope

### Must change

- `pc3d_world::magic`:
  - `Rune` enum (Lumen, Delve) with mana cost and world effect kind;
    `learn(rune)` tracks the learned set.
  - `Mana { current: i32, max: i32 }` regenerating MANA_REGEN per tick
    on the fixed clock, casting deducts.
  - `cast(rune, target_cell) -> Result<CastEffect, CastError>`: casts a
    learned rune at a target cell — Lumen returns a light placement
    (cells to mark), Delve returns cells to clear (composed through the
    P3D-204 edit plan path by callers); unlearned rune or insufficient
    mana refuses.
  - ` Mana regen only while NOT casting this tick.
- Tests: learning gates casting; mana cost deducts and regenerates;
  Lumen produces its light cells; Delve produces its dig cells; unlearned
  or mana-poor casts refuse; determinism.

### Explicit non-goals

- No fireballs/combat magic (combat is P3D-503 melee v1), no spell UI,
  no schools of magic beyond two runes.

## Contracts and invariants

- deterministic behavior: costs and effects pure.
- authority/ownership: `pc3d_world::magic` owns rune semantics.
- performance: O(effect cells).
- player-visible result: the first LEARNED ability that changes the
  world — the magic path exists.
