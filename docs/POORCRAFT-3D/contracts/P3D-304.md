# POORCRAFT 3D Task Contract

## Identity

- Task ID: P3D-304
- Title: Render direction, width, depth, and current from flow records (no particles)
- Stage/gate: P3D-300 (water and environment)
- Owner/model: owner directive "COMPLETE AT LEAST NEXT 20 steps"; z.ai GLM session
- Date: 2026-09-04

## Goal

Rivers render as flowing water purely from flow records: strokes from
each river region's center toward its downstream, width scaled by
discharge, brightness by current (slope), over the biome map — no
particles, no simulation. A wetness accessor exposes river corridors to
future consumers.

## Current truth

- P3D-301/302/303: river graph, flow records, dirty rebuilds. The atlas
  colors river regions flat blue (no direction/width/current).
- `hydro::wetness(gen, r)` exists.

## Scope

### Must change

- `pc3d_world::proof`:
  - `river_stroke_width(discharge: u64) -> f32` — monotonic in
    discharge (1.0..6.0 px), clamped.
  - `current_shade(slope_per_mille: i32) -> f32` — brightness gain
    monotonic in slope (fast water whiter).
  - `render_flow_map(seed, half) -> AtlasImage`: biome base (dimmed),
    then for every river edge a stroke from center to downstream center
    with width by discharge and shade by slope; byte-deterministic.
- `hydro::wetness_at_mm(gen, graph, wx, wz) -> u8` convenience: wetness
  of the containing region (consumer-facing accessor).
- Binary: `--flow-map <seed>` writes the PNG.
- Tests: determinism; stroke-width monotonicity + clamping; current
  shade monotonicity; river pixels differ from the flat-blue version
  (direction/width actually drawn); wetness accessor consistent with
  region wetness.

### Explicit non-goals

- No animation (static proof), no per-cell water, no GPU work.

## Contracts and invariants

- deterministic: byte-identical reruns.
- ownership: rendering stays in proof; consumers use hydro accessors.
- save/load: n/a. network: n/a.
- performance budget: O(regions + edges), milliseconds.
- player-visible result: rivers that visibly FLOW downhill — wider and
  brighter as they gather.
