## Goal

The survival loop closes deterministically: fishing consumes FishStocks
and yields FOOD items into the inventory, eating consumes food and
restores hunger/health, and a contextual onboarding checklist tracks the
first-tree/first-catch milestones.

## Scope

### Must change

- ITEMS catalog gains `fish` (Food { heal: 15 }).
- `pc3d_world::survival`:
  - `fishing_catch(graph, stocks, inventory, region) -> Option<ItemId>`:
    consumes stock (D-007: the river weakens only in FISH, never in
    flow), yields a fish item into the inventory.
  - `eat(inventory, needs, food_id) -> bool`: consumes one food item,
    clears hunger (Needs::eat) — needs via the P3D-404 Needs type.
  - `Onboarding`: ordered milestone checklist
    (first_tree/first_catch/first_build) with `mark`, `is_done`,
    `all_done`, deterministic encode/decode for persistence.
- Tests: fishing decrements stock + adds fish item; eating consumes the
  item and clears hunger; onboarding ordering and persistence.

### Explicit non-goals

- No cooking/smelting, no farming, no UI.

## Contracts and invariants

- deterministic: all pure; catch bounded by stock.
- authority: fishing rules live with FishStocks; eating with Needs.
- performance: O(slots) inventory ops.
- player-visible: catch → eat → hunger clears.
