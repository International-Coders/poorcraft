# NWR-007 — Wilderness Asset Prompt

```text
Implement NWR-007 only. Use the validated asset factory to ship a small,
original wilderness set: three visually distinct trees, three rocks, shrub and
grass clusters, a fallen log, and one biome landmark. Integrate deterministic
biome-aware placement into world generation and render with batching/instancing,
distance culling, wind where appropriate, LOD, and simple collision.

Assets must have source scripts/files, compiled GLBs, manifest records, runtime
consumers, and proof scenes. Do not use a pile of unique draw calls or download
unclear art. Prove stable seed placement, no collision ghosting, LOD transition,
streaming eviction/reload, and performance at the Steam Deck low tier before
moving to settlements.
```
